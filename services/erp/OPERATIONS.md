# SDS operating procedures

## Owner rhythm

Each morning review the owner dashboard, overdue receivables, pipeline next actions, due deliverables, tender deadlines and risk alerts. Review any prepared Gmail drafts in Gmail, adjust them and send personally. Record the sent message reference and next action. An internally prepared record is not a sent email, accepted quote, signed contract, invoice submission or payment.

Each week review forecast cost-to-finish on every engagement, confirmed staff capacity, milestone acceptance, the next four weeks of cash commitments, recurring renewals and unverified prospect details. Compare actual booked income/expenses with invoices collected and company reserves. The displayed bank/cash ledger is not independent proof of available cash or an owner distribution recommendation.

At month end reconcile approved bank/M-Pesa records, review receivables and supplier balances, post reviewed accruals/payroll/tax through the accountant, close the period and retain evidence. Use native Trial Balance, General Ledger, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Accounts Receivable/Payable and bank reconciliation reports. Keep currencies separate until a reviewed conversion is applied.

## Sales to recurring revenue

1. Create a CRM lead with the buyer's organisation, source, problem, decision owner, next activity and evidence. Website enquiries can sync read-only after the existing Worker token is configured privately. Public signup is not verified identity or consent to repeated outreach.
2. Check existing correspondence/drafts and opt-outs before preparing outreach. Record verified recipient and source in SDS Outbound Draft. Use **Save in Gmail Drafts** only after server authorisation, then notify Richard. If the result is uncertain, use **Reconcile Gmail draft**; do not repeatedly press Create. Preserve edits Richard made in Gmail. The existing scheduled revenue task remains draft-only.
3. Qualify the decision, sources/access owner, business outputs, refresh frequency, users, data sensitivity, acceptance process, timetable and commercial budget. Offer an appropriately scoped paid assessment, implementation or recurring service. Public entry prices are floors, not the institutional price list.
4. Create an SDS Estimate, using actual supplier rates and scoped staff allocations. Record scope, exclusions, terms, taxes, FX, acceptance weights and funding assumptions. Request approval; the owner verifies evidence and approves. Download the client PDF from the resulting native Quotation.
5. Record client negotiation and the actual accepted order/signed agreement in SDS Engagement. Attach it privately, identify the acceptance owner, dates, reviewed payment period and warranty boundary. Activate delivery to create Project, Tasks and Milestones. Deposits, if agreed, are native accounting transactions with evidence; do not mark money received from a proposal.
6. Manage tasks, timesheets, purchases, issues and submitted deliverables against the Project. Milestone acceptance requires the actual deliverable, written client acceptance, approver and date. Prepare a draft invoice, review/post it through finance and applicable eTIMS process, then prepare a Gmail draft for Richard to send.
7. Record the real payment with Payment Entry and reconcile the bank statement. Check outstanding balance. Do not overwrite invoice totals to simulate settlement. Corrections use native cancellation/amendment with an audit trail.
8. Cost changed scope separately, record client approval in SDS Variation and revise delivery commitments formally. Do not edit an accepted milestone's fee to add unapproved work.
9. For paid operations, create SDS Retainer with its signed agreement, sources, outputs, capacity, hours, response targets, monthly fee, billing date, end date and renewal notice. Keep included stabilization separate. Optional recurring automation creates one draft invoice per due period and an internal review alert; it never posts or emails it.

## Tenders and experts

Start at the buyer's official procurement portal. Save the private TOR/addenda, authoritative deadline and submission route. Record every mandatory eligibility, legal/financial and team requirement with evidence. A procurement reply permitting submission is not a waiver of qualification requirements.

Use SDS Expert for verified qualifications, relevant/African experience, references, available dates and signed teaming commitments. Record verified cost evidence in a private Rate Card. AI-assisted engineering does not supply a qualifying PhD, firm history or a person's availability. The owner approves a bid only after the checklist is verified. Mark Submitted only with the actual portal receipt; this system does not submit bids automatically.

## Purchasing, people and assets

Use a Supplier master and reviewed quotation for purchases. Create Purchase Order, check cost centre/Project and budget, record delivery, enter Purchase Invoice and match payment evidence. Review Expense Claims before posting. Purchase approvals and cash checks stay with the authorised owner/finance roles.

Create named staff users and Employee records only from verified details. Configure working calendars, leave types/policies, reporting lines and approvers. Staff enter time/expenses and submit leave through native workflows. Payroll uses reviewed salary components, assignments, deductions and account mappings; verify current Kenyan treatment with the accountant before enabling real posting. Staff personal and payroll documents stay private. Do not give contractors payroll or company-finance profiles.

Track equipment as Asset records with custodian, location, purchase evidence and reviewed depreciation settings. Use assignment/return records on joining, role changes and departure. Remove application access and rotate affected integration credentials on departure.

## Support, governance and evidence

Use Helpdesk tickets for incidents and service requests. Assign a team/agent, priority and agreed response policy. Link delivery work to its Project and record diagnosis, action and resolution evidence. Do not promise coverage the signed support scope and staffing cannot provide. Email replies still go through reviewed Gmail drafts.

Record filing/tax/insurance/security/contract renewals as SDS Obligations with the authoritative source, responsible person, due date and lead time. Completed obligations require private evidence. Review risks on their next review date, record mitigation and closure; only the owner accepts a risk explicitly.

Native records retain change history. Attach client contracts, CVs, credentials, datasets and acceptance evidence as private files or private authorised document links. Do not put these records, email addresses, tokens or financial details in GitHub. Keep an independent protected backup copy and rehearse recovery after upgrades and periodically during operation.

### Invoice correction and billing follow-up

A milestone marked Invoiced must reference its own non-cancelled Sales Invoice. Draft invoices remain visible as internal review alerts. Cancelling a milestone invoice clears the milestone link and returns it to Accepted, preserving acceptance evidence and reopening the billing follow-up. Prepare a replacement or use native amendment after reviewing the correction. Invoice provenance cannot be removed to bypass approved terms.

A cancelled retainer invoice retains its period in Cancelled SDS billing period. Native amendment restores that period to the replacement's unique live billing key. The checker flags cancelled periods with no replacement; it does not infer that an invoice should be written off or automatically charge it again.

Website enquiries use the source project's client ID to load the administrator-only registrant record. CRM stores the supplied name, organisation and email, with an explicit unverified-identity note. Existing owner-entered fields are preserved; changed source details are recorded for review. Verify the recipient and any opt-out before preparing outreach.
